# Artwork provenance

The survivor, infected, vehicle, facility and damaged-environment meshes are original procedural geometry authored for this project in `actor_visuals.gd`, `structure_visuals.gd`, `environment_overlay.gd` and `world_art.gd`. The scripts construct their geometry locally; no external asset service is required at runtime.

The interface SVG panel skins and command pictograms in `assets/ui/` are original code-generated artwork. `crew_equipment_atlas.png` and `title_keyart.png` are AI-generated illustrations created for this project, depicting fictional survivors, salvaged equipment and a ruined rail district. They are not photographs of real people or supplied third-party game assets.

This provenance note does not grant a new license to the project or claim that generated artwork is exclusive. Existing bundled font and engine notices remain applicable to their respective components. Audio provenance is documented separately.
