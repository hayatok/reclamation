# Central station defense — implementation checkpoint

This is a new M3 prototype based on the verified city-alley checkpoint, not a recovery of the lost implementation. Only the reclaimed central station (pump site at -16,-14) receives 600 base HP and becomes a target for existing enemies. No enemies or special waves are added. The generator and shelter substation retain their previous restoration behavior; M1/M2 sites are unchanged.

Zero HP disables the station without deleting its node, footprint, reclamation payment or accumulated transmission progress. Workers use the existing repair order, travel, Shift queue and salvage accounting. A disabled station restarts at 25% maximum HP. Base repair is 8 HP/worker-second, costing 0.1 salvage/HP: recovering an unupgraded station from zero costs 15 salvage and 18.75 worker-seconds without travel or attacks. These are provisional prototype values, not validated campaign balance. Armor and instant field repair follow existing building effects; passive regeneration remains restricted to its existing recipients.

The existing site selection shows HP, the objective reports shutdown and retained progress, the existing attack alert says repair is needed rather than falsely reporting destruction, and shutdown dims this station's material instances. Repair references and disabled state survive actual checkpoint serialization. A selected station's command detail shows the remaining recovery cost and existing worker gesture.

Import passed. Controlled tests exercise ordinary commands, damage, multiple-worker payment and actual atomic save/load. The first test run passed 36 checks and exposed two assertions with an invalid assumption that an enemy must attack on the immediately following navigation tick; the test was corrected to observe the actual lethal hit. See the subsequent recorded test result for acceptance, rather than treating the initial failures as gameplay defects.

A native controlled scene with intact M3 terrain already showed five existing boss impacts disabling the station, preserved hold at 25 seconds, and a normal worker repair order reaching the site and adding 64 HP over eight seconds for the existing cost. This is not earned progression. Normal-cost defense/loss/recovery in an earned campaign, final small-screen presentation, real Web/Mac/phone behavior and subjective difficulty remain unaccepted. This checkpoint must not be described as release-ready.

Further bounded verification: all 38 controlled integration assertions pass after correcting the attack timing assumption. Native enemy and worker approaches succeeded with the authored M3 terrain intact. The touch details panel now puts the selected site's existing HP/repair cost information before general shortcuts; landscape and portrait rotation/Close were exercised with synthetic touch. An intact-terrain zero-salvage fixture used normal gather/delivery to obtain 15.6 salvage in22.2 simulated seconds, then returned and repaired to150 HP in24.0 seconds, preserving hold. No enemies were present in this resource-recovery fixture, and no resources were injected. These checks establish a recovery path, not campaign balance.
