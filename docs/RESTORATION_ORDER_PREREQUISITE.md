# Reject unavailable restoration without losing work

An ordinary native economy playthrough found that ordering generator restoration before Age II interrupted three gathering workers, sent them across the base, then rejected the job only on arrival. They remained idle after development completed and required another command.

The command now checks the fixed development prerequisite before replacing worker assignments. Rejected workers retain gathering, cargo, reservations and queued tasks. Mixed-selection fighters may still move to the chosen position. Existing arrival checks remain as a safeguard. Resource costs, development time and eligible restoration are unchanged.

The same earned native save was used for an actual box-selection and right-click replay: the prerequisite notice appeared immediately while the selection still showed 採取中. Saved unit states are exactly equal before and after. The shipped focused test verifies repeated rejection, full checkpoint invariance, mixed-selection movement and normal restoration after Age II. Real phone/browser and first-time human experience remain unverified.
