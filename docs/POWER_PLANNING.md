# Power planning

Electrical coverage appears only while placing a powered building or inspecting a generator/powered facility. Previously a geometrically legal green foundation did not show whether electricity could reach it. The new view shows the current 22 m coverage and required/spare capacity before paying, and separates generator off, range, capacity, manual stop, construction, and pending allocation when inspecting facilities.

The presentation reads committed simulation allocation. It does not change priorities, costs, construction, combat, save data, or placement eligibility. Preview “給電可能” describes current availability, not a reservation or a guarantee after completion. Existing unpowered facilities show required versus currently free capacity; allocation priorities can mean a capacity upgrade powers one consumer at another's expense.

Desktop placement uses a thin dashed coverage ring and nearest-source reference. Mobile retains tap-to-place and adds required/current spare power to its existing mode line. Coverage is depth-tested beneath exploration fog, known-source filtered, and hidden with modals, cancellation, or unrelated selections. Canonical supply-production status strings are unchanged.
