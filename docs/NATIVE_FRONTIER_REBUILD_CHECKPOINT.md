# Native frontier continuation checkpoints

These two unedited saves were produced through ordinary desktop mouse/keyboard play, with normal paid gathering, production and development. They preserve useful starting points for continued player-flow review; they are not a new gameplay version.

The first save follows discovery of the infection source and a focus-attack order by four registered scouts. The second follows their loss during retaliation: infection-source HP is 4740/6000 and HQ remains1000/1000. Home defenders, workers and production remain available for rebuilding. A damaged house was repaired while defenders intercepted enemies, and an earned resource upgrade was selected without losing the scouting selection.

A fixed15fps movie replay of the first save was independently reviewed at normal whole-frame scale. The red glow, retaliation notice and countdown were visible before emerging reinforcements; no high-impact cue flaw was established, so no cue or balance change was adopted. Movie output is not runtime performance evidence. No real phone/browser, audio audition or human-first-time evaluation is claimed.
